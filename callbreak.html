CallBreak Scoring Rules (Click to view)
  • Made the Call: If Achieved tricks ≥ Expected bid, Score = Bid + (Achieved - Bid) × 0.1. (e.g., Bid 3, Won 4 → 3.1).
  • Broken Call: If Achieved tricks < Expected bid, Score = -Bid. (e.g., Bid 4, Won 3 → -4.0).
  • Total Tricks: 4 players share 13 tricks per round (total sum of tricks won should be 13).
  • Mid-score: Sum of Rounds 1, 2, 3, and 4.
  • Final-score: Sum of all 5 Rounds. Highest total score wins!
